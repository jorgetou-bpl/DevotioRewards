<analysis>**original_problem_statement:** 
The user wants to build a custom scanner app for a SaaS whitelabel service, Devotio Rewards. This app needs to connect to the Boomerangme API. The primary requirement is to prevent the display of Personally Identifiable Information (PII) like full name, email, and phone number when a loyalty card is scanned, which is a limitation of the default Boomerang scanner app. The app should display non-sensitive data such as customer ID, transaction status, and loyalty points.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
-   **PII Masking:** Hide customer's full name, email, and phone number on the scan result screen.
-   **Data Display:** Show Card ID, Customer ID, and loyalty status (stamps, points, rewards).
-   **Core Functionality:**
    -   Scan loyalty card barcodes (initially requested as QR codes).
    -   Fetch card and customer details from the Boomerangme API.
    -   Process loyalty actions: add stamps/points, redeem rewards, apply discounts, etc., for various card types.
    -   Display transaction confirmation.
    -   Allow searching for customers.
-   **Branding:** The app should be branded as Devotio Rewards.
-   **Demo Data:** The app must include comprehensive demo data for various customer and card scenarios to be used in a sales proposal.

**User's preferred language**: English
